A 10-step agent plan where every step has a side effect (a row in a SQLite ledger standing in for "email sent", "file uploaded").

1. Worker A runs in a **subprocess**. On step 6 it performs the side effect, then hangs.
2. The test sends **SIGKILL** to worker A, mid-activity.
3. Worker B starts in-process. Steps 1–5 are **not** re-run: their results are in the history. Step 6 misses its 2-second heartbeat, times out, and is retried on B. B finds the effect already recorded under its idempotency key `runId:6` and doesn't repeat it.

Measured result (test): **10 results, 10 effects, 11 attempts**, attempt order `1 2 3 4 5 6 6 7 8 9 10`. It runs on Temporal's local dev server (`WorkflowEnvironment.start_local()`), so it's a real server and a real process kill, and takes about 14 seconds.
