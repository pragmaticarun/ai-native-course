## Intuition

Closer to home: what makes **an engineering team** get value from AI? The best-known answer is DORA's, and it's deliberately *not* a ladder.

## DORA AI Capabilities Model (2025)

From the 2025 State of AI-assisted Software Development research. The core finding: **"AI is an amplifier"**. It magnifies a team's existing strengths and weaknesses. Seven capabilities amplify the benefits:

1. Clear and communicated AI stance
2. Healthy data ecosystems
3. AI-accessible internal data
4. Strong version control practices
5. Working in small batches
6. User-centric focus
7. Quality internal platforms

DORA also published an ROI framework and calculator for AI-assisted development (Apr 2026).

## Thoughtworks Technology Radar, Vol. 35 (Nov 2026)

Not levels, but a useful read on practice. Themes include harness engineering at scale, **keeping human judgment as agents multiply** (warning against "human-in-the-loop theater"), choosing beyond frontier models, and AI economics. The Adopt ring includes **agent sandboxing** and threat modelling.

## Try it

Score your team 0–3 on each DORA capability. Keep the numbers: the [worked example](/ai-native-course/maturity/worksheet/) turns them into a radar chart.

## C++/Rust notes
- "AI is an amplifier" is the same lesson as "a faster compiler doesn't fix a flaky test suite": small batches, strong version control and good platforms are what let speed turn into delivery.
