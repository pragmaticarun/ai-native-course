<Aside type="note" title="Whose ladder is this?">
The levels are Dan Shapiro's (a practitioner's blog post, January 2026). The capabilities are DORA's (AI Capabilities Model, 2025). **Combining them into entry criteria for each level, and the 0.8 review-load threshold, is this course's own synthesis**, not a published standard, as the syllabus promised. Argue with it; that's the point of writing it down.
</Aside>
