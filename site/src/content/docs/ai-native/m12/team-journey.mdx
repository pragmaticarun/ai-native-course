(The test asserts these verdicts and findings.)

## What happened, week by week

- **W01.** Leadership wants agents everywhere. 30 agent PRs a week at half an hour each against 12 reviewer hours is a review load of 1.25: the team would rubber-stamp. Eight prerequisites are missing too.
- **W04.** The team writes AGENTS.md, gets the test suite under two minutes, and agrees on small PRs. L2.
- **W09.** PR policy and a verify command exist, but there's no eval suite for the prompt-driven features and no shared platform (each engineer runs agents differently). Not yet.
- **W12.** An eval suite gates prompt changes, a shared runner config is the platform, and a fourth reviewer rotation brings review load to 0.56. L3.

## C++/Rust notes
- A systems team's W09 blocker is often the platform: shared sanitizer/fuzz CI and a build cache. Without them, agent PRs are slow to verify and reviewers fall back to reading every line.
