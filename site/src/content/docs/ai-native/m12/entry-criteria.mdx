## DORA's seven AI capabilities

DORA's [AI Capabilities Model](https://dora.dev/ai/capabilities-model/report/) (2025) names seven capabilities that amplify the benefits of AI:

1. Clear and communicated AI stance
2. Healthy data ecosystems
3. AI-accessible internal data
4. Strong version control practices
5. Working in small batches
6. User-centric focus
7. Quality internal platforms

DORA doesn't map them to levels. That mapping is ours.

## The course's entry criteria

<Aside type="caution" title="This is our synthesis">
Neither Shapiro nor DORA nor Thoughtworks publishes this table. It's the course's judgement of what should be true *before* a team moves up, so the move pays off instead of amplifying problems. Change it for your context; keep the habit of writing gates down.
</Aside>

| Move to | DORA capabilities first | Team artifacts first |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | clear and communicated AI stance | an AI policy (what tools, what data) |
| L2 | strong version control practices; working in small batches | AGENTS.md; a fast test suite |
| L3 | AI-accessible internal data; quality internal platforms | a PR policy (L11.4); an eval suite; one verify command |
| L4 | healthy data ecosystems; user-centric focus | specs; skills; sandboxes; agent budgets |
| L5 | — | not a target here |

Plus one check from L3 up: **review load**, agent PRs × review hours per PR ÷ reviewer hours, must stay at or below 0.8. Thoughtworks Radar Vol. 35 advises assessing review capacity before scaling agent use; the 0.8 number is ours.

`gaps(team, target)` lists everything missing level by level, so skipping a level shows every prerequisite you'd skip. `ready_for` adds the review-load check.

## Thoughtworks Radar Vol. 35 cautions (November 2026)

- **Human-in-the-loop theater** (Caution): approvals humans can't realistically evaluate. Use risk-tiered oversight, mutation and property-based testing.
- **Coding throughput as a measure of productivity** (Caution): it rewards busywork.
- Keep human judgement as agents multiply; review and delivery pipelines become bottlenecks.

## C++/Rust notes
- For systems teams, add "sanitizer and fuzzing jobs in CI" to the L3 artifacts: they're the platform capability that makes agent PRs reviewable.
