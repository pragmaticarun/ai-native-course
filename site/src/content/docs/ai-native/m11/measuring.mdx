"We ship 2× more PRs since adopting agents" says nothing about whether users get better software sooner. Measure delivery, and measure what can break.

## DORA's five metrics

From [dora.dev](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/) (last updated 5 January 2026):

| Group | Metric |
|---|---|
| Throughput | change lead time · deployment frequency · failed deployment recovery time |
| Instability | change fail rate · deployment rework rate |

Add one team-level signal DORA doesn't define but agents stress first: **review latency** (PR opened → first review).

DORA's 2025 research describes AI as an **amplifier**: it magnifies a team's existing strengths and weaknesses. Faster throughput with rising instability is the pattern to watch for.

## Before and after, on a fictional team

The test generates 12 weeks before (3 deploys/week, 10% fail, 4 h review) and 12 weeks after adopting agents (6/week, 25% fail, 9 h review). `compare` reports:

- `amplifier warning: throughput up, instability up`
- `review is the new bottleneck: latency up more than 50%`

and with only 8 deployments in a window: `too few deployments to compare (need 20 per window)`.

<Aside type="caution" title="Invented data, course rules">
The team and its numbers are made up to exercise the code. The comparison rules (minimum 20 deployments per window, the 50% latency threshold) are this course's choices, not DORA's.

## Pitfalls

- **Metrics as goals.** DORA's guide warns against turning them into targets; teams game what they're judged on.
- **Activity metrics.** PR counts, lines of code, % of code written by AI. Thoughtworks Radar Vol. 35 cautions against coding throughput as a measure of productivity.
- **Confounders.** A new hire, a release freeze, a big migration. Compare windows of similar work, and say what changed besides agents.
- **Too little data.** A small team deploying twice a week needs months to see a real change in fail rate.

## C++/Rust notes
- Add language-specific instability signals next to DORA's: sanitizer findings per week, new `unsafe` blocks, fuzzing crashes. If agent adoption raises them, that's rework you'll pay later.
