## Intuition

GUI agents need the same discipline as the AndroidWorld and OSWorld benchmarks:

- each task has **setup → run → state check → teardown**;
- the grader reads **app state** (here the simulator's `db`; on a device, `adb shell` or content-provider queries; in a browser, the DOM), **never the agent's claim**;
- runs are **seeded**, and you report **pass@1** and **pass@k**. pass@k uses the unbiased estimator from Chen et al. 2021, [arXiv:2107.03374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374): `1 − C(n−c, k) / C(n, k)`.

The 15 tasks cover forms (with late layout shifts and prefilled fields), settings toggles (including "already set: do nothing"), search, two prompt-injection tasks, a shop with a "Pay now" trap, and an **infeasible** task where the right answer is to say so.

Metrics per run: success, steps (actions), invalid-action rate, unsafe attempts (consequential clicks outside the task's allow-list, blocked or not), illustrative cost, and wall-clock time.

<Aside type="caution" title="Simulator only">
Everything runs against an in-process simulator. Running these tasks on an Android emulator (self-hosted KVM runner) or the Galaxy A73 is **not done** in this milestone.
</Aside>

## Results (5 seeds per task, k = 3)

```text
naive         {'pass@1': 0.507, 'pass@3': 0.533, 'mean_steps': 2.93, 'invalid_action_rate': 0.089, 'unsafe_attempts': 10, ...}
careful       {'pass@1': 0.88,  'pass@3': 0.993, 'mean_steps': 5.41, 'invalid_action_rate': 0.033, 'unsafe_attempts': 4,  ...}
careful+guard {'pass@1': 0.92,  'pass@3': 1.0,   'mean_steps': 5.41, 'invalid_action_rate': 0.033, 'unsafe_attempts': 4,  ...}
```

Read it like this. The naive agent fails every layout-shift, injection, prefilled and infeasible task, and its 10 unsafe attempts are the two injection tasks × 5 seeds. The careful agent's *slips* (a seeded 25% chance of missing a click by 50 px) sometimes land on **Pay now**: 4 unsafe attempts, a lesson in itself. With [M5's consequential-action guard](/ai-native-course/fundamentals/m5/gui-safety/) those clicks are **blocked** (still counted as attempts) and the cart task passes on every seed.

## Try it
1. Lower `slip` to 0.1 and raise `max_steps` to 8. How do pass@1 and pass@3 move, and what does that say about step budgets?
2. Add task 16: a toggle that is *disabled* (greyed out). What should success mean?

## C++/Rust notes
- setup/teardown per task ≈ test fixtures that restore an emulator snapshot, like a fresh container per integration test.
- pass@k ≈ flaky-test statistics: "passes at least once in k retries" is not "reliable".
