## Intuition

You can't unit-test a stochastic function with one assertion, but you can test its **behaviour over a dataset**. An eval is:

- a **dataset** of cases (inputs, plus references or properties);
- **graders**, from cheapest to most expensive: *exact* match → *programmatic* (contains, regex, parses, tests pass) → *LLM-as-judge* (a rubric, used only when nothing mechanical works, and calibrated);
- a **suite runner** that reports a pass rate and the failing cases, run in CI against mock models, and nightly or before release against live ones.

Treat the suite as a **regression gate**: a prompt or model change that drops the pass rate doesn't merge.

## C++/Rust notes
- Evals ≈ property-based tests and fuzzing: many inputs, assertions about properties, results as rates.
- A regression suite in CI ≈ a benchmark gate (`criterion` with a threshold): don't merge slowdowns, or here, quality drops.
