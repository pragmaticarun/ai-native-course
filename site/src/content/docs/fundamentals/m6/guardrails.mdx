## Intuition

Guardrails sit at the edges:

- **In:** length limits, schema validation, injection heuristics (for flagging and triage);
- **Tools:** a per-task allow-list and argument rules, checked *before* anything runs;
- **Out:** secret and PII detection and redaction before text leaves the system or reaches logs.

<Aside type="caution">
Pattern-based injection detection is easy to evade. The structural defences matter more: least privilege, per-task tool policies, human checkpoints for consequential actions, and treating tool, web, email and screen content as **data**. Exercise 2 fixes an agent that obeys an email.
</Aside>

## C++/Rust notes
- Validate at the boundary ≈ `serde` with `deny_unknown_fields` plus newtypes: once inside, data is trusted *because* it was checked.
- Luhn-checked card detection ≈ checksumming before acting on a value.
