## Intuition

The pipeline: a **planner** groups commits into sections → **workers** draft each section → a **human** reviews → a **publisher** (the only component allowed to write) publishes. Publishing is mocked: it records the notes and sends nothing.

The gate is built against rubber-stamping:

- the reviewer sees a **unified diff** against the last published notes;
- approving requires a reason of **three or more words**, and the decision must name a reviewer;
- rejections and blocked decisions are logged with the exact diff shown.

## What the tests check
- a reasoned approval publishes v1, and the reviewer saw `+- Add AV1 decode` / `-- Old` in the diff;
- an "ok" approval is **blocked**, a rejection is honoured, and nothing is published in either case.

## C++/Rust notes
- The publisher as the only writer ≈ a single-owner resource (`&mut` held by one component); everything else gets `&`.
- The audit log ≈ an append-only journal you can replay to see who approved what, and what they saw.
