Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play in the park near her house. Every morning, she put on her red boots and ran outside to see the ducks in the pond. The ducks were her friends. She gave them bread and they quacked happily.
One day, Mia saw a small brown dog sitting under a big tree. The dog looked sad and lonely. Mia walked over slowly and said, "Hello, little dog. Are you lost?" The dog wagged its tail and licked her hand. Mia smiled. She wanted to help the dog find its home.
Mia and the dog walked around the park together. They asked a man with a hat, but he did not know the dog. They asked a lady with a blue bag, but she did not know the dog either. Mia felt worried. Then she saw a boy crying near the gate. The boy looked up and shouted, "Max! You came back!" The dog ran to the boy and jumped into his arms.
The boy said thank you to Mia. His name was Leo. Leo and Mia became good friends. After that day, they played in the park together with Max every morning. They fed the ducks, climbed the little hill, and laughed all day long.
Tim was a boy who liked to build things. He had a box full of blocks, sticks, and string. One rainy day, Tim decided to build a boat. He used the sticks for the sides and the string to tie them together. His mom gave him a piece of cloth for the sail.
When the rain stopped, Tim took his boat to the stream behind his house. He put it in the water and watched it float. The boat moved slowly at first, then faster and faster. Tim ran along the side of the stream, cheering. "Go, boat, go!" he said.
But then the boat hit a rock and got stuck. Tim tried to reach it, but it was too far. He felt sad. Just then, a big frog jumped onto the rock. The frog pushed the boat with its strong legs, and the boat was free again. Tim laughed and waved at the frog. "Thank you, frog!" he said. The frog croaked and jumped back into the water.
Lily had a garden with many flowers. There were red roses, yellow sunflowers, and tiny purple violets. Every day, Lily watered her flowers and talked to them. She believed that the flowers could hear her. One morning, she found that one flower was drooping. Its petals were dry and its leaves were brown.
Lily asked her grandpa what to do. Grandpa looked at the flower and said, "This flower needs more sun. Let us move it to a better place." They dug up the flower carefully and planted it near the fence where the sun was bright. Lily gave it fresh water and some soft soil.
A few days later, the flower stood tall again. Its petals were bright and pink. Lily jumped with joy and hugged her grandpa. "You were right!" she said. Grandpa smiled and said, "Every living thing needs the right place to grow." Lily remembered those words for a long time.
Ben the bear lived in a big forest. He loved honey more than anything else. One day, he found a tall tree with a beehive at the top. Ben wanted the honey, but the bees were buzzing loudly. He thought for a moment. Then he had an idea.
Ben went to the river and picked some sweet berries. He brought the berries to the bees and said, "I will share my berries if you share your honey." The bees talked to each other. Then the queen bee said, "That is a fair trade." Ben put the berries under the tree, and the bees gave him a little pot of honey.
Ben was very happy. He sat by the river and ate his honey slowly. He learned that it is better to ask nicely and share than to take things that belong to others. From that day on, Ben and the bees were good neighbors, and they shared berries and honey every summer.
Sara wanted to see the stars. Her mom said she could stay up late on Saturday. When the night came, Sara and her mom took a blanket outside and lay down on the grass. The sky was dark and full of tiny lights. Sara counted the stars, one, two, three, but there were too many to count.
Suddenly, a bright light moved across the sky. "Look, Mom! A shooting star!" Sara said. Her mom told her to make a wish. Sara closed her eyes and wished for a puppy. She did not tell anyone her wish, because she wanted it to come true.
The next week, her dad came home with a box. Inside the box was a small white puppy with black spots. Sara screamed with happiness. She named the puppy Star, because it reminded her of that special night under the sky.
